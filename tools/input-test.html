Loudness range—LRA

Loudness is how loud it sounds, not how high the peaks go: the measure broadcasters and streaming services use. Talk for 20–30 s and read Integrated. For reference: −23 broadcast (EBU R128), −16 podcasts and spoken word, −14 music streaming. A raw microphone usually reads lower; a call app's auto gain levels it. A mono input counts as heard on both speakers.
